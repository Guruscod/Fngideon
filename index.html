About Me

Design with a purpose. Content with a voice.

I'm Gideon Norkplim Fiadowu, a Ghana-based creative who enjoys turning simple ideas into visuals and content that people remember. I trained through ALX's Creative track and Virtual Assistant programme, and I have worked with small businesses and brands on design, websites and day-to-day content needs.

I like to keep things simple: clear communication, clean design, and work that is delivered on time. Whether you need a logo, a landing page, a month of social posts, or an extra pair of hands to manage your inbox and schedule, I bring the same attention to detail to every task.
